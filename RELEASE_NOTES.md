# Release Notes

Version v3.20.0 — October 4, 2026

## Incoming Skills by Player

- New section under Defense showing which enemy skills are hitting each squad member. Use the Damage/Casts toggle to switch between how much damage a skill did and how many times it was cast at that player.
- NOTE: Cast counts only exist for arcdps builds from May 2026 on. Older fights are counted as unmeasured rather than zero, so they won't drag your numbers down.

## Fixes

- Fixed the Incoming Damage Modifiers table having broken column alignment on web reports.
- Stats tables (like Defense detailed) now fill their split pane vertically instead of leaving dead space.
