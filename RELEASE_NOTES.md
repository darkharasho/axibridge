# Release Notes

Version v3.22.8 — October 17, 2026

## Mark Players on the Replay
Right-click anyone on the replay map, friendly or enemy, to mark them as a tag or with one of four shapes. Each side gets one of each.
- Marking the enemy commander opens a card that compares their speed with your tag's, shows the distance between the two, and estimates who's leading and by how much.

## Edge of the Mists on the Replay
EotM fights now get a proper map: tiles, keeps, towers, camps and sectors, instead of a blank canvas.

## APM Counts Real Presses Only
APM no longer counts weapon swaps, attunement and legend swaps, shroud/forge/avatar toggles, engineer kits, Firebrand tomes, or entries that aren't presses at all (pet spawns, relic and rune triggers, buffs ending). Kit and form classes were inflated against everyone else. Expect noticeably lower APM for Holosmith, Scrapper, Amalgam, Firebrand and elementalists.
NOTE: Skill Usage still lists all of them.

## QoL Improvements
- Distance to Tag now defaults to median, so a few far-off moments don't drag the number up.
- Player names in stats tables are left-aligned.

## Fixes
- Fixed the replay scrubber jumping while dragging near the start of a fight.
- Fixed clicking a player on the replay picking the wrong one when the map was zoomed or panned.
